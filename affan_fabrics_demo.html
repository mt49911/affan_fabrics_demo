ABOUT THE BRAND

Tradition Meets Modern Elegance.

AFFAN FABRICS.NG, also known as Menlace Plug | Bespoke, is a Nigerian textile and fashion brand focused on luxury men's fabrics, bespoke wear and traditional garments.

Located in the renowned Kwari Market, Kano, the brand aims to make it easier for customers to discover fabric collections, ask questions and place order enquiries online.
